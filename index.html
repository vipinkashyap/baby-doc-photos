US-born · Indian parents

Baby's first official photos

One good picture of your baby → correctly sized files for every application. Everything runs on your phone; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1Where a photo is (and isn't) needed

US Passport (Form DS-11) Photo
2×2 in printed — bring 1 printed photo to the acceptance facility. White background. Baby's eyes may be partially closed (under 1 yr). Use the 4×6 print sheet below at CVS/Walgreens (~35¢ vs $17 studio).
OCI Card Photo
51×51 mm square, JPEG 200–900 px, ≤ 200 KB, plain light background. Also needs baby's left thumb impression in the signature box (not a signature). 2 printed copies if applying via VFS.
India e-Visa Photo
Square JPEG, 350–1000 px, 10–300 KB, white/light background. Faster than OCI for a first India trip — you can do e-Visa now, OCI later.
Regular Indian Visa (VFS) Photo
Same 2×2 in / 51×51 mm square, digital upload ≤ 300 KB + 2 printed copies. Only if not doing e-Visa/OCI.
Birth Certificate No photo
Ordered from the county — no photo needed.
Social Security Number No photo
Usually auto-applied at the hospital — no photo needed.

2Take or upload the photo

Drag to position · pinch or slider to zoom. Fit their head between the orange lines (crown to chin), face centered.

Baby tips: lay them on a plain white bedsheet, shoot from directly above in daylight, no shadows, no hands/toys/pacifier in frame. Eyes ideally open; partially closed is OK under 1 year.

Before you submit
  • Photo taken in the last 6 months (babies change fast — retake for each new application)
  • Plain white/light background, no shadows or patterns
  • Face straight-on, fully visible, nothing else in frame
  • Same photo not reused across applications months apart
  • OCI: don't reuse an Indian-passport-style 35×45 rectangle — it must be this square
Sizes per US Dept. of State, ociservices.gov.in and indianvisaonline.gov.in specs as of mid-2026 — double-check the portal's stated limits when you apply, they occasionally change. All processing happens locally in your browser.
