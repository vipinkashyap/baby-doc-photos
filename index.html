Baby's first official photos

One good picture of your baby → correctly sized files for every application. Everything runs on your phone; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1Take or upload the photo

Baby tips: lay them on a plain white bedsheet, shoot from directly above in daylight, no shadows, no hands/toys/pacifier in frame. Eyes ideally open; partially closed is OK under 1 year.

2Mark the crown and chin

Drag the two orange lines onto the top of the head and the bottom of the chin. Drag the photo itself to centre the face; pinch or use the slider to zoom.

You only do this once — each file below is then cropped to its own official head-size rule automatically.

Before you submit
  • Photo taken in the last 6 months (babies change fast — retake for each new application)
  • Plain white/light background, no shadows or patterns
  • Face straight-on, fully visible, nothing else in frame
  • Same photo not reused across applications months apart
  • OCI: don't reuse an Indian-passport-style 35×45 rectangle — it must be this square
Which documents need a photo?
US Passport (Form DS-11) Photo
2×2 in printed — bring 1 printed photo to the acceptance facility. White background. Baby's eyes may be partially closed (under 1 yr). Use the 4×6 print sheet above at CVS/Walgreens (~35¢ vs $17 studio).
